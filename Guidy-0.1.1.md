
The first public beta of Guidy.

- Ask "how do I…" and Guidy now points at the first thing to click and walks you through anything longer than one click, without you having to say "step by step".
- Guidy is in beta, so its version now starts at 0.1.
- Hold Control + Option and ask out loud how to do something. Guidy looks at your screen, answers, and points at the right button.
- Ask "how do I..." and Guidy stays with you step by step until the task is done.
- The intro now plays Guidy's own short demo, from inside the app.
- If Guidy is missing a permission when you ask, it says so and opens its panel, where each one has an Allow button.
- Setting up is smoother: each permission turns to "Allowed" the moment you allow it, the panel counts "2 of 4 done", and one Restart Guidy button finishes Screen Recording.
- If an old copy of Guidy is in the way of a permission, Guidy says so and a Fix it button clears it.
- Guidy starts speaking sooner: answers and lesson steps are voiced as they are written.
- The panel has a new paper look.
- Scam guard is smarter: an AI model now looks at a risky screen and decides, so reading a chat or a guide that shows Terminal commands no longer sets it off. It only marks a scam when it is sure and can point at the proof.
- New under More: Delete my Guidy data, Share anonymous usage stats (on or off), Acknowledgements.
- Guidy tells you, before it asks for anything, what it sees and where it goes.
